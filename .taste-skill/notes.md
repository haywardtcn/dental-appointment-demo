## Argument (copy.md §1)
- Reader: someone in Hong Kong looking for a dentist, often for a specific problem (wisdom tooth, missing tooth).
- One-screen belief: this is a calm, real clinic in Causeway Bay, and I can book now.
1. It is a dental clinic in Causeway Bay, open now?  -> clinic photo, live open-now status, Book button
2. The room is clean and calm                         -> two clinic photos
3. It does surgery, implants, cosmetic, family care  -> service rows, each pre-fills the booking form
4. A hospital-trained oral surgeon treats you         -> portrait, three credentials, bio
5. Booking takes a minute                             -> form + hours + address

## Idea
On this page, the clinic's live opening hours become the one live thing (open-now status in hero and contact, today's row highlighted).
Competitor test: any clinic could show its hours, so the idea is the category's. Creativity capped at 7.
